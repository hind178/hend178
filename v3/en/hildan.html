Identity / business card

Hildan: a two-sided card
that mirrors itself.

Client
Hildan Holding Group
Piece
Two-sided business card
Size
3.5 × 2 in (89 × 51 mm)
My role
Card design
01

Project Overview

Hildan is a holding group in investment.

02

Design Challenge

The business card is the first thing a partner or client receives, so it has to say «investment, building, trust» on a palm-sized piece, without clutter.

03

Design Approach

  • Just two colours: earthy gold (about #C4A668) with black on the front, and white with the same gold on the back.
  • The logo alone on the front: the drawn H mark and the HILDAN name in the centre, with HOLDING GROUP in widely spaced letters below. Nothing competes with it.
  • Two symbols for the field: cracks like earth or stone, and rising building shapes in faint transparency — a nod to construction and growth without literal photos.
  • Two sides as a mirror: on the front, cracks on the left and buildings on the right; on the back they swap — cracks on the right, buildings on the left. Turning the card over feels like the same piece.
  • One system for contact details: each line has a round gold icon — phone, email, location — with text in the same colour, and a vertical gold line beside the name and title.
  • Three typefaces by role: Montserrat, Hammersmith One and Aileron, one for each level of text.
04

Final Deliverables
