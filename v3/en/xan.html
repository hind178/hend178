01

Project Overview

A joint offer from the Xan Bazar market and the Hil 12 group in Duhok: customers shop, collect points, and turn them into shares.

02

Design Challenge

The idea has three stages and two audiences — Kurdish and Arabic readers — and the roll-up has to explain it to someone standing in front of it for one minute.

03

Design Approach

  • Two languages, equal weight: the headline in Kurdish on the right and Arabic on the left, at the same size, divided by a vertical line. Neither is the «main» language with the other a translation.
  • The idea as steps, not a paragraph: shop, collect points, invest — three stops from top to bottom, joined by arrows.
  • Each step has a colour and an icon: navy for the Xan Bazar logo, orange for a hand receiving a star, green for a rising arrow. Colour alone tells the steps apart.
  • The same colours in the frame: green and orange in the top corners, navy and orange in the bottom bands, so the whole roll-up uses just three colours.
  • Two lines per step: Kurdish in white inside a navy tag, Arabic in black beneath it in larger type.
  • Two typefaces: Speda for Kurdish and Arabic, and Garet for the Latin name Hil 12.
