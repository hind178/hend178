About me

About me

I'm a graphic designer from Duhok. I work on things that get printed and held — bottle labels, product bags, cartons, roll-ups, business cards and booklets — and on company profiles and website interfaces.

Most of my work has to speak more than one language. So I care about fitting Arabic, Kurdish and English onto the same surface without crowding — and about the mandatory details like ingredients and barcodes.

On the pieces shown here, that means a bottle label with separate English and Kurdish faces, a roll-up that gives Kurdish and Arabic equal weight, and a website that flips fully from English to Arabic with one toggle.

Areas of expertise

  • Graphic DesignColours, type and a key image chosen to suit each product and audience.
  • Packaging & Label DesignBottle labels, snack bags and cartons, with ingredients, nutrition and barcodes placed clearly.
  • Corporate ProfilesCompany profiles, presentations and proposals that make information easy to compare.
  • Digital DesignWebsite interfaces for desktop and mobile.
  • Multilingual DesignArabic, Kurdish and English on one piece, in right-to-left and left-to-right layouts.
  • Print ProductionDesigning at real size, checking text and margins, and delivering the final file.

How I work

  1. BriefThe product, the audience, the size and the languages needed.
  2. DirectionColours, type and a key image that suit the product.
  3. DesignLaying out content and mandatory information at real size.
  4. Print-readyChecking text and margins, then delivering the final file.
