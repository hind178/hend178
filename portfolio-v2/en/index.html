How I work

  1. Brief

    Step 01

    The product, the audience, the size and the languages needed.

  2. Direction

    Step 02

    Colours, type and a key image that suit the product.

  3. Design

    Step 03

    Laying out content and mandatory information at real size.

  4. Delivery

    Step 04

    Checking text and margins, then delivering the final file.

Why work with me?

Most of my work has to speak more than one language. I care about fitting Arabic, Kurdish and English onto the same surface without crowding — along with every mandatory detail.
