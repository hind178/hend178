Document / project proposal

Play & Learn: an idea
in well-ordered pages.

Type
Proposal for a children's centre
Format
A4, 10 pages
Language
Arabic
My role
Writing and full design (2025)

Context

Play & Learn is the idea for a centre for children aged 4 to 10 that combines mental and physical play with behavioural learning. The proposal has to convince a reader — a partner or a funder — that the idea is well thought out: what the sections are, what it costs, when it opens, and what the risks are.

Decisions

  • One recurring shape: the hexagon frames the cover photos, appears faintly in pastel colours behind every page, and returns on the back cover. The ten pages feel like a single piece.
  • A fixed page template: a navy heading at the top, then text, then one or two photos at the bottom. Readers always know where to look.
  • Numbers in two columns: the budget and timeline are laid out as item against figure, so they read like a table without table lines.
  • A typeface per role: a distinctive Arabic display face for the cover title, a classic Latin serif for the year in coral red, and Noto Sans Arabic for the body text.
  • A back cover that closes the story: a vertical navy band with the title, in the same colour as the inside headings.

Limits

This is a proposal for a project idea, not an operating centre. The figures and durations in the pages are estimates from within the proposal, not results. The images are from the design file.
