عامٌ يشبهني — My Planner 2027

Type
My own product — a yearly planner
Format
A5, 86 pages
Language
Arabic, with small English subtitles
My role
Concept and full design

Context

A 2027 planner I designed as my own product. It opens with personal pages — about me, the year at a glance, goals and vision — gives every month a calendar, a monthly plan and weekly pages, and closes with notes, sketch pages and a letter to the future self. A planner is used every day for a whole year, so its 86 pages have to stay calm, consistent and easy to write in, while still feeling personal and warm.

Decisions

  • A soft palette: blush pink with lilac and peach, a gold four-point star, and flowers that return on the cover and the inner pages.
  • Arabic first, English as a whisper: each heading in bold Arabic with a small italic English line beneath it, and large numerals for months and weeks (01, W02).
  • Talking to the reader: the cover addresses her directly — «خطّطي · احلمي · تألّقي» (plan · dream · shine).
  • One rhythm for every month: a month opener with the calendar, then the monthly plan, then one page per week. The month openers rotate between pink, lilac and peach.
  • Room to write: rounded boxes with ruled lines, checkboxes for goals, a 31-day habit tracker, mood circles, and a water tracker on every weekly page.
  • A local calendar: month names as used in Iraq (كانون الثاني، شباط…), weeks that start on Sunday, and a note that Hijri occasions are approximate.
