Context

Badinan Soft is a software company in Duhok, operating since August 2007, with many products for different sectors. The roll-up stands at an exhibition or reception, where visitors walk past quickly — so they need to grasp who the company is, what it offers and how to reach it, in that order.

Decisions

  • The logo at the top: the first thing visible from a distance and above people's heads, with the company line «where your needs lie» beneath it.
  • Colours taken from the logo: the logo's red and dark grey become slanted triangles framing the top and bottom, leaving a white middle for reading.
  • One heading system: ABOUT US, OUR PRODUCTS and CONTACT US in the same bold type, with the second word always in red.
  • Products as icons: ten products shown by their logos in a two-row grid, instead of a long text list.
  • Contact within reach: phone, email, social accounts and a QR code in the lower third, at eye level for someone standing close.

The eye's path

  1. 01Logo and line — who we are, in a phrase.
  2. 02ABOUT US — two paragraphs on the company.
  3. 03OUR PRODUCTS — the grid of logos.
  4. 04CONTACT US — phone, email, QR.
  5. 05The bottom band — website and address.

Limits

These images are from the design file, not photographs of the printed roll-up. The design uses the company's logo, its product logos and its own introductory text.
