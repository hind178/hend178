# My Grandmother's Tales — Episode 01 · سفرة ما چانت بالحسبان
## 5-second style test · Tilt-Shift Miniature · 1080×1920 · 30 fps

### Three candidate structures
1. **The continuous dive (chosen).** One unbroken camera move: a high time-lapse god's-eye of the campus bus → speed drop to real time → land at Hind's eye level → the band widens to take in the two friends. It uses the style's two strongest native moves (the dive, the speed drop) and fits the theme: of all the busy little world, the story is about one small person.
2. **Band relay.** Locked high frame; the sharp band slides from the bus, to the luggage, to Hind, to the friends. Elegant, but the faces stay tiny — a bad test of the characters.
3. **Real time first.** Open close on Hind's hands on the suitcase handle, then lift. Ends wide — but the brief asks for the three-shot as the final image.

Chosen: 1, because the test must prove both the world (wide) and the dolls (close) in 5 s.

### Logline and arc
A shy student waits alone by the trip bus, unsure — until two girls step out of the crowd and smile at her.
Setup (wide, time-lapse bustle) → turn (time slows; Hind alone, uncertain) → ending (Loreen smiles, Hind turns, pleasantly surprised; three-shot).

### Benchmark
Learn: tilt-shift time-lapse — high vantage, blur band, saturated sunny paint, stepped crowds, smooth camera.
Don't learn: any real place, landmark, brand or logo. The campus, sign and bus are fictional.

### Shot list (one continuous shot)
| t | Framing | Camera | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | high oblique wide (~50° pitch) | very slow slide + start of descent | the whole campus as a model; students swarm the bus in ×20 stutter |
| 1.5–3.0 | dive → medium on Hind | crane down + push in, band narrows onto her | "the one small thing that matters"; real time arrives |
| 3.0–5.0 | medium → medium-wide three-shot | gentle pull back + arc left | the friends enter the band; end on the composition |

### Beat sheet
- 0.0 campus wide, crowd stutter at 8 Hz, world ×20.
- 1.2–2.4 speed ramp ×20 → ×1, stutter fades into smooth motion.
- 1.8 Hind sharp in the band, blinking; looks toward the bus, slight downcast brows.
- 2.4 Loreen and Lizette step out from the bus door side and walk toward her.
- 3.4 Loreen sees Hind, smile grows. 3.7 Hind's head, then body, turns; eyes widen, brows lift, soft open smile.
- 4.3 friends stop; 4.3–5.0 settle, breathing, blink, final composition.

### Sound
This test is silent by request (no narration). The full episode will follow the style's sound palette: tuned percussion in time-lapse, a tape-stop into real-time ambience (birds, distant engine idle, footsteps) at the speed drop.

### Type
None in this test (no subtitles). The only lettering is the university's gate sign, part of the world.
